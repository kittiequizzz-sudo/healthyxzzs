# Scene plan: "¿Qué pasa si dejas de cenar 7 días?"

About 2,860 words, roughly **19 minutes** at 150 wpm (a calm pace for a senior audience). There are about 300 beats; each
**section** below lists its scene sequence. Timing comes from the voice-over (transcribe → 0.1–0.3 s lead).

The format is the approved one: code infographics, plus real drawings in a few places. This video is about organs, not
exercise, so it needs **no pose sheets**, only an **organ sheet** and **scene stills** (static, push-in only).

## Visual system for this video
- **Organ map** (recurring): a senior silhouette with the 6 organs (from ORDER A). The organ being discussed lights up
  and the others fade; it comes back at every section change as "progress".
- **Night clock** in the corner (18:00 → 03:00 → 07:00) during the "while you sleep" parts.
- **"Día 1 … Día 7" counter** in the intro and the conclusion.
- Recurring colours: green = possible benefit, amber = depends, red = risk/warning.

## Punto 1, estómago e intestinos (≈2.6 min)
- Title card + organ map (stomach + intestines).
- **MMC diagram**: intestine illustration from ORDER A, an orange wave travelling along it, labeled "complejo motor migratorio · cada 90–230 min".
- "¿más tiempo sin comer = más sano?" → large "NO NECESARIAMENTE" card.
- Two columns: green "menos pesadez" / red "hambre, náuseas, gastritis".
- Autophagy: cell icon with a recycling arrow + amber "beneficio clínico: aún desconocido" label.
- Subscribe callout (small bottom card).
- Case of the 68-year-old: still S2 (cold-cut/bread/cheese dinner late at night) → still S3 (light, early dinner); clock 23:00 → 20:00.
- "2–3 horas antes de dormir": timeline bar showing dinner → bed.
- Transition: liver lights up.

## Punto 2, hígado (≈2.6 min)
- **Warehouse diagram**: the liver from ORDER A, with a glycogen battery draining over the night (clock).
- Fuel arrows: GLUCÓGENO → GRASAS / GLUCONEOGÉNESIS (labeled).
- "no está eliminando toxinas" → red X over a "DETOX" label.
- Calories vs nutrients: balance scale "menos calorías ✓" vs "menos proteínas ✗".
- Warning card: diabetes / hígado / medicamentos → "consulta profesional".
- Share callout. Transition: brain lights up.

## Punto 3, cerebro (≈2.7 min)
- Still S4: senior awake at 3:00 a.m. in a dark bedroom; clock 03:00.
- Pie chart "≈ 1/5 de la energía en reposo".
- Fuel icons: GLUCOSA → (long fast) CUERPOS CETÓNICOS.
- **Hypoglycemia symptoms card** (sweating, tremor, palpitations, nightmares, confusion), in red.
- Sleep: hypnogram with frequent awakenings vs a calm one.
- "olvidar unas llaves ≠ lesión" (keys icon).
- Comment callout. Transition: heart.

## Punto 4, corazón (≈2.6 min)
- Heart from ORDER A pulsing; counter "≈100.000 latidos/día".
- Night blood-pressure curve with the "dipping".
- Bar "−4 mmHg (sobre todo con pérdida de peso)", source TRE 2022/2025.
- Still S5: senior getting up at night toward the bathroom, dizzy → "riesgo de caídas".
- Electrolytes Na / K / Mg as 3 tiles.
- **Emergency red card**: dolor torácico / dificultad respiratoria → "URGENCIAS".

## Punto 5, músculos (≈2.6 min)
- Scale: −kg split into AGUA / GRASA / MÚSCULO (stacked bar).
- Functions: walking, balance, getting up from a chair (3 icons).
- "Sarcopenia": muscle from ORDER A shrinking + "≈50% de mayores de 80" (Harvard).
- Still S6: senior with a calf cramp in bed.
- Protein foods: still S7 (eggs, fish, legumes, dairy) + "1–1,2 g/kg/día (ESPEN)".
- Share callout + "ejercicio de fuerza".

## Punto 6, páncreas (≈2.7 min)
- Pancreas from ORDER A; animation: carbs → glucose → insulin opening "doors" into a cell.
- Overnight insulin curve falling.
- "No hace falta eliminar la cena": 4 tiles (calidad, ejercicio, descanso, peso).
- Case of the 70-year-old on insulin: still S8 (checking a glucose meter at night) → hypoglycemia symptoms → "EMERGENCIA".
- Morning: glucose curve after a refined breakfast (amber, "depende").

## Conclusión (≈2 min)
- Organ map, all 6 lit, with a one-line summary each.
- Red warning card (>60, diabetes, medication → consult).
- "Mejora tu cena": still S3 again + 3 checks (verduras, proteína, temprano).
- **Quiz**: 3 cards HÍGADO / PÁNCREAS / ESTÓMAGO, pancreas lights green after a pause.
- Like/subscribe end card + final quote in large type.
