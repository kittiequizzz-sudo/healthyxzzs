# Prompt blocks (paste into every image prompt; English for the image app)

Owner's rules (Oct 21 2026): diverse cast, adult-animation look, strong expressions, fewer repeats.
ChatGPT gives landscape 3:2 only; Gemini also does 16:9. Always end with "no text".

## STYLE (scene panels)
> STYLE: adult animated series look for grown-ups (not a kids' cartoon): realistic adult proportions,
> head about 1/7 of body height, angular faces with visible age (wrinkles, sagging skin, age spots),
> bold clean ink outlines, flat cel shading with one shadow tone, muted grown-up palette, film-like
> framing with close-ups and varied camera angles, simple uncluttered backgrounds.

## COLOR (owner, Oct 30 2026: the Oct 26 "deep but harmonious" look came out too neutral and didn't
## attract views → vivid, high-contrast, still NO yellow/golden cast)
> COLOR: vibrant, saturated, high-contrast palette: cobalt blue, emerald green, crimson red, coral, fuchsia-pink
> and bright teal accents over deep navy shadows; crisp cinematic lighting with strong contrast and a cool blue
> rim light so the people pop off the background; rich clean colours, never muted, grey or washed-out;
> neutral-to-cool white balance with NO yellow, orange or sepia cast and no golden glow.
Metaphor panels: "each panel on its own bold, saturated gradient background (deep cobalt, teal, plum or
crimson)" with "COLOR: vibrant, saturated, high-contrast colours: crimson and coral for the heart and
blood, cobalt and bright teal for nerves and air, emerald and fuchsia accents; glowing highlights and crisp
shading…; NO yellow, orange or sepia cast". Item sheets keep the flat cream #F5ECD7 (needed for cutting).
Old (Oct 26, retired): "rich, deep but harmonious palette of deep navy, teal, forest green, burgundy…".
Don't attach images from the older (yellow) videos as style references.

## EXPRESSION (one per panel, pick and adapt)
- effort: teeth gritted, brows knitted, veins on the forehead, one hand pushing on the knee
- worry: brows raised in the middle, lips pressed, eyes looking down, hand at the mouth
- surprise: eyes wide, mouth open, hand on chest, leaning back
- pride: big grin, chin up, chest out, fist raised
- relief: eyes closed, long exhale, shoulders dropped, small smile
- pain: wincing, one eye shut, hand on the knee or lower back
- doubt: one eyebrow up, side glance, hand rubbing the chin
- joy: laughing with the head back, eyes squeezed shut

## CAST (spell each person out; vary across panels)
Examples for a Spanish-speaking 60+ audience:
- a tall thin man of about 70, bald with a white goatee, dark brown skin, cardigan and slacks
- a short heavy-set woman of about 65, light skin, grey hair in a bun, glasses on a chain, floral blouse
- a stocky man of about 68, tan skin, thick grey moustache, flat cap, plaid shirt
- a slim woman of about 75, olive skin, short curly white hair, pearl earrings, tracksuit
- an Indigenous woman of about 70, braided grey hair, embroidered blouse, shawl
- a man of about 80 with a walking cane, hearing aid, light skin, sweater vest

## AVOID (end of every prompt)
> AVOID: cute or chibi proportions, baby faces, big round kid eyes, glossy 3D or toy look, the same
> person in every panel (unless asked), any text, letters, numbers, speech bubbles, signs or logos.
