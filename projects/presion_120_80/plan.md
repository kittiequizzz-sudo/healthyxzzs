# Scene plan: "¿Sigue siendo 120/80 la cifra perfecta?"

~3,030 spoken words → ~20 min. Sections = `intro`, `punto1`…`punto6`, `cierre` (one HTML each, shared
`common.js`, built like `projects/7dias_sin_cenar/`). Style: `youtube-style.md` (picture every beat, ≤ 6-word
captions, hook cut every 1.5–2.5 s).

Drawings: 9 scene sheets (2×2 = 36 panels) + 2 item sheets (20 items) + reuse from earlier videos
(`7dias_sin_cenar/cut`: heart, brain, fish, lentils, veg; `azucar_sube/cut`: cafe, pesas) ≈ 60 drawings,
about 1 per 20 s.

Characters: **Don Andrés** (`characters/senior_es`, replaces senior_m; natural skin, coral polo: the hook's 70-year-old, measuring scenes, habits) · **Señora** (unnamed,
82: hook + orthostatic scenes) · **Elías** (69, new) · **Natividad** (86, new). Fictional people get the chip
"Dramatización" / "Ejemplo".

Panel names = `<sheet>_<n>` in reading order (see `order.md`):
- `senior_a`: 1 calm on sofa with cuff · 2 health magazine · 3 nurse measures him at clinic · 4 arguing on phone
- `senior_b`: 1 worried at monitor · 2 pill organizer · 3 asks the doctor · 4 two men same age (active vs frail)
- `senora_a`: 1 dizzy rising from armchair · 2 sitting on bed edge, moving feet · 3 standing up holding furniture · 4 losing balance, grab rail
- `senora_b`: 1 glass of water · 2 telling the doctor · 3 lying on exam table with cuff · 4 standing with cuff
- `elias`: 1 workshop (violin) · 2 morning walk · 3 calm check-up · 4 pill at breakfast
- `nati`: 1 walker at home · 2 wrist bandage after a fall, daughter · 3 doctor reviews medicines · 4 bookbinding bench memory
- `mal`: 1 just came in with bags, out of breath · 2 cuff + coffee · 3 slouched, arm hanging, legs crossed · 4 tense at clinic
- `bien`: 1 resting upright, feet flat · 2 arm on table at heart level, silent · 3 writing in notebook · 4 shows notebook to doctor
- `vida`: 1 walking in park · 2 band exercise seated · 3 balance at chair · 4 garden with grandchild

Recurring visuals (code):
- **BP readout**: monitor cut-out with a blank screen; digits drawn over it. Every number on screen comes from code.
- **Target gauge**: systolic scale 100–180, green band 120–129, red line at 140 (diagnosis), amber zone below 110
  with a dizzy icon. Returns in P2, P4, cierre.
- **Progress strip** (top right): 6 icons (mito · cifra · baja · edad · medir · hábitos), current one ringed orange.

## intro (~85 s, a change every 1.5–2.5 s)
- Cold open `senior_a_1` → digits slam "135/80" + chip "70 AÑOS · Ejemplo" → cut `senora_a_1` → "115/65" + "82 AÑOS"
  + dizzy stars.
- Split screen of both stills, caption "¿QUIÉN ESTÁ MEJOR?".
- "120/80" stamped over `senior_a_2` (magazine) → `senior_a_3` (clinic). Caption "¿LA CIFRA PERFECTA?".
- Preview, 3 pictures ~1 s each: gauge · `senora_a_4` · `mal_3`.
- Open loop: gauge with the band hidden behind "?" + "PUNTO 2".
- Promise: `vida_4`, caption "Sin falsas promesas".

## punto1: el mito (~170 s)
- Kicker "PUNTO 1 · EL MITO" over `senior_a_2`. Gear icon ✗ → day curve of BP rising/falling with icons (emoción,
  movimiento, descanso, pastilla).
- Heart cut-out squeezes → top number lights "SISTÓLICA"; relaxes → bottom number "DIASTÓLICA".
- `arteria_flexible` stretches with each beat vs `arteria_rigida` barely moves; systolic digits climb. Caption
  "ARTERIAS MÁS RÍGIDAS".
- Stamp "EXPLICAR ≠ ACEPTAR". Risk row pops per word: brain (ictus) · heart (insuficiencia) · `rinones` (daño renal).
- One reading: `mal_1` (stairs/bags) → `senior_a_4` (phone) → `senior_b_1` (worry), each with an ↑ on the digits.
- Reverse: good digits + `senora_a_1` → amber "?".
- Takeaway (green, 2 short lines): "REDUCIR RIESGO · Y TOLERARLO".
- `senior_b_2` → "CONTROLADA ≠ CURADA"; red pill "No lo suspendas por tu cuenta".
- Bridge: empty gauge with "?".

## punto2: la cifra (~200 s)
- Kicker "PUNTO 2 · LA CIFRA". Source line "Guías europeas de cardiología (ESC) · 2024". Gauge band fills, count-up
  "120–129" big, caption "SI SE TOLERA BIEN".
- Age chips 65 · 70 · 80 slide onto the band. Stamp "LA EDAD NO LO JUSTIFICA".
- Tolerance: dizzy/faint icons with amber ring.
- Three concepts placed on the gauge (not cards): normal (low zone) · diagnóstico ≥ 140/90 (red line) · objetivo
  120–129 (green). Bubble «Estoy por debajo de 140…» ✗.
- Elías (Dramatización · 69): `elias_1` → `elias_2` → `elias_4` → `elias_3` + digits "125/75" ✓.
- Natividad (86): `nati_4` → `nati_1` → `nati_2` (chip "varias caídas") → `nati_3`. Side by side, caption
  "ESTADO, NO SOLO EDAD".
- "≥ 85 o fragilidad": amber band widens on the gauge, caption "OBJETIVO MENOS ESTRICTO".
- "135/80": two badges "no es urgencia" / "no es el ideal".
- Comment CTA over `bien_3`: bubble "¿En casa o en consulta?".

## punto3: presión baja (~170 s)
- Kicker "PUNTO 3 · DEMASIADO BAJA". Bubble "Cuanto más baja, mejor" ✗.
- Term "HIPOTENSIÓN ORTOSTÁTICA" over `senora_a_1`.
- Mechanism on the `silueta` cut-out (objetos #11): blood dots drift to the legs on standing (gravity arrow) → vessels squeeze and heart
  speeds up (normal) → slow response → dizzy.
- Symptom row pops per word: mareo · visión borrosa · debilidad · desmayo.
- Falls: `senora_a_4` → chips fractura · hospital · autonomía.
- "HAY QUE INVESTIGAR"; causes: `vaso_agua` (deshidratación) · `pastillero` · brain.
- Safe stand-up = 3-step sequence with numbered step cards: `senora_a_2` (sit, move feet) → `senora_a_3` (stand with
  support). Caption "DESPACIO".
- "Si se repite → médico": `senora_b_2`.
- Clinic test: `senora_b_3` (lying) → `senora_b_4` (standing), chip "tumbado → de pie".
- Verdict: same digits, ✓ without symptoms / ✗ with fainting.

## punto4: 65, 75, 85 (~170 s)

- `senior_b_4`: same age, same digits → two different gauge bands.
- Doctor checklist icons: heart/brain (infarto, ictus) · `rinones` · drop (diabetes) · pills · walking/balance.
- Balance scale: "beneficio (tarda)" vs "efectos (antes)".
- Fit → band 120–129; frail → wider band "más cautela".

- Subscribe CTA (red pill) over `vida_1`.
- Bridge: `mal_3` with "?" on the digits.

- Move: `vida_1` → `vida_2` → `vida_3`, caption "MODERADO Y REGULAR".

- `vida_4`, caption "SIN MIEDO".

  sanitario" · 5 symptom icons → "LLAMA AL 112".

- Quiz: A "siempre 120/80" (`senior_a_2`) · B "adaptada y tolerada" (`vida_4`). No answer revealed. Comment bubble;
  like · subscribe · bell.
- Closing quote on ink: "Cumplir años no es dejar de cuidarte. Es cuidarte mejor."
