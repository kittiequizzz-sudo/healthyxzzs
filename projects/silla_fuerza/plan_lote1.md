Style: cartoon panels (`references/cartoon-style.md`): full-screen doodle panels on flat pastel colours,
hand-lettered titles and labels in code, people differ per panel (all 60+). Exact times come from the
voice cues. Budget: 24 panels (6 sheets of 2×2) + 1 pose sheet for the test, plus code scenes.

Section colours: intro = mixed; Punto 1 = lavender; Punto 2 = mint. Sheets ask for the colour per panel.

## Punto 1 — caminar no basta (~3 min), lavender
- Title card over C1: "PUNTO 1 · CAMINAR NO BASTA".
- 10.000 pasos: C1 couple walking + step counter; benefits row as three doodle chips: CORAZÓN · CIRCULACIÓN · ACTIVO.
- "y aun así perdiendo fuerza": code bar "FUERZA" slowly draining next to the walker.
- Sofa after a walk: C2 (punch on the hands). Label "SE IMPULSA CON LAS MANOS".
- "no significa que estés enfermo": soft green chip "NO ES ENFERMEDAD", amber "SEÑAL".
- Caminar ≠ fuerza: split screen C1 | C2 with "≠".
- Terreno llano, intensidad moderada: code intensity meter (low/medium/high) needle at medium for walking, high for stand-up.
- Levantarte, subir escalones, equilibrio: C3 stairs → A4 rug.
- Sarcopenia: code muscle bars by decade shrinking; caption "SARCOPENIA". Source EWGSOP2 2019.
- Movimientos rápidos / reaccionar al tropezar: A4 (catching balance), C4 getting up from the floor, D3 sitting up in bed.
- Car analogy: D1 car on flat road ("MOTOR CONSTANTE") → D2 car accelerating uphill ("ACELERAR").
- "no abandones tus paseos": C1 + green ✓ "SIGUE CAMINANDO" + "+ FUERZA".
- Silla estable: B1 again.
- Comment CTA: D4 woman typing on a tablet + bubble "¿CUÁNTOS MINUTOS CAMINAS?".
- Bridge "una prueba de 30 segundos": code 30 s timer pops.

## Punto 2 — la prueba de la silla (~2:50), mint
- Title over F1: "PUNTO 2 · LA PRUEBA DE LA SILLA". Big timer "30 s".
- "sin gimnasio ni aparatos": B2 again (short).
- Name of the test + "profesionales": F2 professional with stopwatch; caption "PRUEBA DE 30 SEGUNDOS"; amber chip "NO ES UN DIAGNÓSTICO". Source CDC STEADI; Jones 1999.
- Antonio (Dramatización): E1 walking proudly ("67 AÑOS", km counter) → E2 pushing up from sofa ("AMBAS MANOS") → E3 low chair at grandchildren's ("SILLAS BAJAS") → E4 thinking: "RESISTENCIA ✓ · FUERZA ✗".
- How to do it: F1 chair against the wall + "NO RESBALA"; pose sheet as a step sequence: 1 seated (arms crossed, feet flat, hip width) → 2 lean → 3 rise → 4 standing tall; code timer counts 30 s and a rep counter ticks on each stand. Labels: "PIES APOYADOS", "BRAZOS CRUZADOS", "SOLO REPETICIONES COMPLETAS".
- Safety card (hold ≥ 8 s): F3 dizzy woman + red card "DOLOR · MAREO · DESEQUILIBRIO → NO FUERCES", "MEJOR CON SUPERVISIÓN".
- "no es competir… edad, sexo": two chips "EDAD" "SEXO" + "NO ES UNA COMPETICIÓN".
- Hopeful news: F4 very old man doing the stand with a coach clapping; caption "MEJORA A CUALQUIER EDAD". Source Fiatarone 1990.
- Subscribe + bell CTA (code, red pill + bell).
- Bridge "qué ocurre dentro de tus músculos": muscle doodle glowing (code) → next round.
