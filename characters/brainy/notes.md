# brainy: owner's brain mascot ("2D Minimalist Brain Character Explainer")

`sheet.jpg`: 5 poses (thinking, idea, reading, strong, thumbs) cut into `cut/` with
`cut_items.py sheet.jpg cut --names thinking,idea,reading,strong,thumbs`. White round head with an exposed pink
brain, white body, thick black ink, flat pastel, no shading, off-white background #F7F6F2. Attach this sheet to every
prompt with the mascot. In scenes: show poses as cut-outs with pop/slide/float, never rotate limbs.
Style test: `projects/partes_cerebro/demo_estilo.html` (bubbles with ink outline, pink highlight #E0707A, yellow chips).
