# Scene plan: "Lo que hace tu cerebro mientras duermes" (Intro + Punto 1–2)

About 630 words, roughly **4–4.5 min** at ~150 wpm. Ten points are promised; this plan covers the part
received. Timing comes from the voice-over (transcribe → cues 0.1–0.3 s before the word).

Format: code infographics + real drawings shown as stills (push-in) and cut-outs. No pose sheets
(no exercise). Drawings: ORDER A (brain/item sheet) + ORDER B, C, D (2×2 scene panels) = 16 drawings
for ~4.3 min ≈ 1 per 16 s. Character: round_m (white round-head man, `characters/round_m/`).

## Visual system
- **Night palette**: ink `#1B1A22` sky with a moon in the corner for "while you sleep"; cream for the
  diagrams. Same colours as the style bible (red = risk, amber = caution, green = OK).
- **Night clock** in the corner (23:00 → 03:00 → 07:00) moving through the video.
- **Progress strip**: 10 small numbered circles top right ("1 … 10"), current one ringed orange.
- **Hypnogram** (code): the sleep-stage staircase (DESPIERTO / REM / N1–N3). Reused to show
  "fases profundas" (N3 highlighted) and "fase REM" (REM blocks highlighted red).

## Punto 1 — el cerebro se limpia (≈1:50)
- Kicker "1 · LA LIMPIEZA NOCTURNA" over the brain cut-out (≤ 1.5 s).
- "residuos… millones de neuronas": neuron cut-out with small grey particles drifting off (code dots).
- "sistema glinfático": **diagram** (code): a blood vessel in cross-section with the blue CSF sleeve
  flowing along it, arrows into the tissue, grey particles carried away. Caption "SISTEMA GLINFÁTICO".
- "fases profundas": hypnogram, N3 blocks glow blue.
- **City metaphor**: still B2 (night city, street-sweeper truck washing the street) → crossfade to the
  brain with the blue flow. Caption "LIMPIEZA NOCTURNA".
- "líquido cefalorraquídeo… vasos sanguíneos": back to the vessel diagram, label "LÍQUIDO CEFALORRAQUÍDEO".
- "beta-amiloide… Alzheimer": amber clumps (code) on the neuron; caption "BETA-AMILOIDE"; then an
  amber stamp "AÚN EN ESTUDIO" (for "todavía se investiga… en las personas"). Source line Xie 2013 (ratones) · Fultz 2019.
- "dormir no es solo descansar": still B3 (man waking rested, morning light) with a green check.
- Subscribe CTA: red pill + the 10 circles with "1" ticked, "quedan 9".
- Bridge "impedir que muevas tu cuerpo": cut to B1 with the red lock.

## Punto 2 — la parálisis que te protege (≈1:50)
- Kicker "2 · EL CANDADO DEL SUEÑO" over C1.
- "corriendo, saltando, escapando": still C1 (asleep, dream bubble: running from a dog) — split screen
  with B1 (body still in bed). Caption "SUEÑO ≠ MOVIMIENTO".
- "atonía muscular… fase REM": hypnogram, REM blocks in red; eye icon with fast flicks (code). Captions "ATONÍA" / "FASE REM".
- "tronco encefálico… inhiben las neuronas": brain sagittal cut-out (A2), brainstem ringed red, a
  signal arrow running down the spinal cord to a muscle with a "STOP" gate.
- "GABA y la glicina": 2 molecule chips ("GABA" · "GLICINA") sliding onto the gate. Source Brooks & Peever 2012.
- (if the RBD line is added) "¿y si el bloqueo falla?": still C2 (man kicking/punching in his sleep, wife
  startled) → amber card "TRASTORNO DE CONDUCTA DEL SUEÑO REM · consulta al médico".
- "no se sincroniza con el despertar": two timeline bars, CONSCIENCIA turns on before MÚSCULOS → gap in red.
- "parálisis del sueño… presencia invisible": still C3 (man awake, eyes open, frozen, faint shadow at the
  door; not gory). Caption "PARÁLISIS DEL SUEÑO".
- "unos segundos… inofensiva… aterradora": seconds counter; green chip "SUELE SER INOFENSIVA". Source Sharpless & Barber 2011.
- Bridge "sacudida repentina": still C4 (man jolting awake as he falls asleep, motion lines) → cut.
