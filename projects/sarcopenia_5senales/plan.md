# Scene plan: "5 señales de que estás perdiendo músculo" (sarcopenia)

~3,000 spoken words → ~20 min. Sections: `intro`, `senal1` … `senal5`, `conclusion`, built like
`projects/ictus_6pasos` (one HTML per section, shared `common.js`, `youtube-style.md` rules: a picture on every
beat, ≤ 6-word captions, a cut every 1.5–2.5 s in the hook). Audience: Spain, 60+, "tú".

Characters: **the viewer / Manuel (69, Valencia)** = `characters/senior_m`. **Antonio (66, Sevilla)** = a new
character (bald with white side hair, white moustache, round glasses). Doña Carmen's design appears as Manuel's
wife in two panels. Antonio and Manuel scenes carry the chip "Dramatización".

Recurring pieces (`common.js`):
- **Progress strip** (top right): 5 sign icons: mano · silla · pasos · báscula · escalera; current ringed orange.
- **Sign kicker:** "SEÑAL n · …" orange pill over the section's first picture.
- **"Señal ≠ diagnóstico" chip** (amber) whenever the script says "no significa necesariamente".
- **Exercise card:** step sequence from the pose sheets, numbered steps, progress dots, rep counter
  ("8–10 ×", "5 ×", "8–15 ×"), red callout on the working muscle, green "SIN DOLOR" chip.
- **Urgent card** (red, held ≥ 8 s).

Drawings: 7 scene sheets (28 panels, A–G) + 2 pose sheets (P1, P2: 8 poses) + 2 item sheets (H, I: 15 items) + reuse
`eggs`, `fish`, `lentils`, `dairy`, `body` from `projects/7dias_sin_cenar/cut`. ≈ 1 drawing per 20 s, the rest code.
Cut names: A `senales` s1–s4 · B `manos` m1–m4 · C `silla` c1–c4 · D `pasos` p1–p4 · E `bascula` b1–b4 ·
F `escaleras` e1–e4 · G `final` q1–q4 · P1 `sit_to_stand` sts1–4 · P2 `talones` tr1–4 ·
H `objetos` (bote, bolsas, sarten, pelota, bascula, banda, zapato, pesas, pantalon) ·
I `musculos` (mano_musculos, pierna_cuadriceps, gemelo, muslo_joven, muslo_mayor, celula_mito).
