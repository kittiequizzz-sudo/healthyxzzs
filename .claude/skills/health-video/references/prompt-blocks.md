## EXPRESSION (one per panel, pick and adapt)
- effort: teeth gritted, brows knitted, veins on the forehead, one hand pushing on the knee
- worry: brows raised in the middle, lips pressed, eyes looking down, hand at the mouth
- surprise: eyes wide, mouth open, hand on chest, leaning back
- pride: big grin, chin up, chest out, fist raised
- relief: eyes closed, long exhale, shoulders dropped, small smile
- pain: wincing, one eye shut, hand on the knee or lower back
- doubt: one eyebrow up, side glance, hand rubbing the chin
- joy: laughing with the head back, eyes squeezed shut
